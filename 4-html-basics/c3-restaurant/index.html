Maquis du Chateau - Menu du jour

Entrées

  • Attiéké œuf

    Steamed cassava semolina topped with boiled eggs, diced tomatoes, onions, and enough Maggi to make your ancestors proud. Simple, classic, undefeated.

  • Gbofloto galette

    Golden, pillowy fried dough balls that pair dangerously well with morning gossip. Warning: impossible to eat just one.

  • Pain haricot

    Crusty baguette stuffed with slow-cooked, spicy red beans. The ultimate street-food heavyweight that keeps you full until tomorrow afternoon.

Plats de résistance

  • Attiéké poisson thon

    Crispy fried tuna served on a mountain of fresh attiéké with alloco, tomato-onion salad, and a green chili pepper that will test your bravery.

  • Riz avec sauce tomate et queue de bœuf

    Fluffy white rice smothered in a rich, savory tomato stew with melt-in-your-mouth oxtail. Guaranteed to induce an immediate post-lunch nap.

  • Spaghetti comme chez Diallo

    Stir-fried kiosk-style spaghetti tossed with veggies, sardines, and secret street-chef magic. If you know, you know.

Desserts

  • Petit bissap - glacé cœur

    Chilled hibiscus flower juice infused with mint and pineapple—so cold it will freeze your worries right along with your brain.

  • Dèguè - en d'une manière

    Creamy sweetened yogurt mixed with millet pearls and a touch of vanilla. Served "en d'une manière" (extra special), because standard just isn't our style.
